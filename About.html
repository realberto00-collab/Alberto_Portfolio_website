I'm Alberto Re

• I consider myself an artist, designer, activist, and musician!
• I'm a Miami native, being born in Nicaragua.
• Currently studying for my BFA in graphic design at New World School of the Arts.

• I've been actively screen-printing since 2022, generally within education circles.

• I've created graphics for my friends in the local Miami scene, mixing my illustrative skills with digital, along with engaging proactively with student orgs at FIU, such as the ICEBREAKERS, the YDSA, and Dream Defenders.

• I've been running a personal screenprinted T-shirt brand named EYESWEETS, and selling at vendor spots whenever I have the chance.

• My work has garnered lots of attention, with my artwork being displayed at many art shows, and my designs being displayed on the Washington Post!

Qualifications & Program Proficiencies

• I use a mix of 2D & 3D graphic design software. Some of these programs include the Adobe Suite, Canva, Photopea/Vectorpea, and Cinema 4D.
• I am able to "rip" images, and seperate them into color layers for spot color, and halftone color screenprint jobs.
• Able to code HTML, CSS, and JS (this website showcases some of those skills.)
