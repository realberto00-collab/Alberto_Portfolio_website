Miami Book Fair Poster (2025)

This proposed poster was made in spring 2025.
Inspired by Miami and its buildings, I wanted to stack books ontop of eachother, to resemble the feeling of seeing the emerging buildings in Miami, and their grand scale. The colors are that of the Miami Book Fair color pallete.

The logo being repeated on the cover, is a proposed logo that combines a palm tree, and the current Miami Book fair logo as of 2026.

This was mocked up in Cinema 4D, using cel shading techniques, to then being completed in illustrator to be print ready.

T-shirt Flap Brochure

This Brochure was created at NWSA, Spring 2025.
Made to advertise my small screen-printing operation, and prompted to make a custom diecut prototype, I decided to create a foldable T-shirt brochure. I used the main colors of my brand, its logo, and cut out the prototype, creating a bold conversation piece.

When folded outwardly, It acts as a little stand aswell.

Font Dice

This dice was created at NWSA, Spring 2025.
The inspiration behind this was to use 6 fonts, with each side including a unique typographic design, being made into a rollable dice. I used the fonts in the following order:

1. Impact
2. Lobster
3. Rockwell
4. Wing Dings,
5. Sign Painter
6. Georgia

Marketing Zine

This Zine was created at NWSA, Spring 2025.
I used a common motif in my work, being the use of texture, with all the graphics on this zine to keep a concrete "rustic" feeling. Each page of the zine was meant to be simple, to the point, but maintain a simultaneous visually interesting & true to my style design.
