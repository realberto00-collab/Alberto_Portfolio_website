AP 2D "SI"

This sustained investigation was created at Arthur & Polly Mays Conservatory of the Arts, throughout 2023.
My AP theme was that of growing up as a young male exposed to the internet, and its effects.

These are all large scale pieces, created using a mix of acrylics, markers, color pencils, and oils.
